    About

    Welcome to Rohadas Blog — a space where I share my personal experiences, my thoughts, and the things I am building.

    I created this blog to document my journey and to connect with like-minded people who share my passions and interests. I believe everyone has a story worth telling, and I hope these posts inspire you to share yours too.

    My name is Chirag Rohada, and I am an entrepreneur. In my free time I love to explore new places, so you will find those stories under Travel, alongside the projects I am working on under Work.

    What you will find here

    • Travel — places I have been, and what I learned along the way.
    • Work — the products, experiments and side projects I am building.

    I believe life is a journey, and we all have something to learn from each other. So whether you are here to gain new insights, to share your own experiences, or simply to connect, I invite you to join me and become part of the Rohadas community.

    Thank you for visiting, and I hope you enjoy reading these posts as much as I enjoy writing them.

    You can also reach me on Instagram or Twitter, or subscribe to the newsletter from the header above.
